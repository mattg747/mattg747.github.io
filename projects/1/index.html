Project 1: Prokudin-Gorskii Colorization

Overview

We reconstruct color photographs from digitized Prokudin–Gorskii glass plates by splitting the scan into three monochrome bands (top→bottom = B, G, R), aligning G and R to B, and stacking to form RGB. I implemented a single-scale baseline and a multi-scale pyramid (coarse→fine). To satisfy the rubric, results are reported with NCC (and optionally L2/SSD). Any enhanced methods (edges, weighting, etc.) are shown later under Bells & Whistles.

Method / Approach

  • Preprocessing: read, convert to float64 in [0,1], split B/G/R, and hard pre-crop to remove thick borders.
  • Scoring (rubric): NCC/ZNCC (and optionally L2/SSD) computed on an interior crop (~10%) to avoid wrap/edges.
  • Alignment:
    • Single-scale baseline: exhaustive integer search over a window (e.g., ±15) for small JPGs.
    • Image pyramid: downscale by 0.5 to min side ~250 px; align at the coarsest (slightly larger window), then at each finer level double the shift and refine in ±5–7.
  • Post: stack (R,G,B) → RGB, small border trim (2–3%) to hide seams; save as JPG/PNG (no TIFFs uploaded).
Issues & fixes (high level)
  • Rainbow ghosting: reduced with interior crops and (in extras) edge-based scoring + small full-res refinement.
  • Border bias: hard pre-crop + interior crop; optional center weighting (extras).
  • Large shifts in TIFFs: handled via coarse→fine pyramid.

Approach (for rubric)

Multi-Scale Pyramid Alignment (with NCC / L2)

Step-by-step (for rubric)

  1. Build pyramid by downscaling by 0.5 until min side ≈ 250 px.
  2. At the coarsest level, search a slightly larger window (e.g., ±8–10) with NCC (or L2); record best (dx, dy).
  3. Move one level finer: double (dx, dy) and refine in a small window (±5–7).
  4. Repeat until full resolution; apply final shifts to compose RGB.

Bells & Whistles (Not used for rubric scoring)

  • Edge-based scoring: ZNCC on Sobel / Gaussian gradient magnitude.
  • Hard pre-crop: automatic plate-border trimming.
  • Center-weighted NCC: Gaussian weighting to de-emphasize borders.
  • Full-res local refine: ±2 pixel refinement after the pyramid.

Per the rubric, I still document NCC/L2 results above. These enhancements are for quality and speed.

Notes & Runtime

  • Parameters are fixed across images (no per-image hand tuning).
