Project 1: Prokudin-Gorskii Colorization

← Back to Portfolio

Overview

We reconstruct color photographs from digitized Prokudin-Gorskii glass plates by splitting the scanned plate into three monochrome images (B, G, R from top to bottom), aligning G and R to B, and stacking to form an RGB image. I implemented a single-scale baseline, a coarse-to-fine pyramid for large .tif files, and edge-based scoring to reduce chromatic ghosting. All outputs shown here are compressed (JPG/PNG); large .tif inputs are not uploaded.

Method / Approach

  • Preprocessing: read, convert to float64 in [0,1], split B/G/R, and hard pre-crop to remove thick borders.
  • Scoring metric: Zero-mean Normalized Cross-Correlation (ZNCC). For robustness, I also support ZNCC on edge features (Sobel or Gaussian gradient magnitude). I compute scores on an interior crop to avoid wrap/edges.
  • Alignment:
    • Single-scale baseline: exhaustive search over a window (e.g., ±15) for small JPGs.
    • Image pyramid: downscale by 0.5 to a min side ~250 px; align at the coarsest level, double the shift each level, and refine in a small window (±5–7). Optional larger window at the coarsest level (±8–10).
  • Post: stack (R,G,B) → RGB, small border trim (2–3%) to hide seams; save as JPG.
Issues & fixes
  • Rainbow ghosting on edges: mitigated by aligning on edge features (Sobel / Gaussian grad) and a final ±2 pixel full-res refinement.
  • Border bias: fixed with hard pre-crop + interior metric crop + optional center weighting of the score window.
  • Large displacements (.tif): handled by the coarse-to-fine pyramid; small fixed window per level keeps runtime low.

Failure / Difficult Cases

If an image failed or shows residual color fringing, here’s why and what I tried.

  • Thick borders: required hard pre-crop plus interior metric crop.
  • Low-texture regions: alignment ambiguous → edges or gradient-SSD scoring helped.
  • Repetitive textures (e.g., melons): small coarsest-level window caused wrong basin; increasing to ±8–10 fixed it.

Bells & Whistles

  • Edge-based scoring: ZNCC on Sobel and Gaussian gradient magnitude.
  • Hard pre-crop: automatic plate-border trimming.
  • Center-weighted ZNCC: Gaussian window to de-emphasize borders.
  • Full-res local refine: ±2 pixel refinement after the pyramid.

Notes & Runtime

  • Parameters are fixed across images (no per-image hand tuning).
  • Typical runtime: < 1 minute per large image with the pyramid (laptop).
  • All outputs here are compressed JPG/PNG for the report.
